# Slide 3 — Signatures are representations too

**Timing:** 8–11 minutes  
**Module:** Signatures and the file-centric literature

## On-slide content

- Cryptographic hashes
- Byte sequences and wildcards
- Strings and structural conditions
- Behavioral rules

> A signature encodes an expert-selected invariant.

Strengths:

- Cheap
- Interpretable
- Precise when the invariant is stable

## Speaker notes

The weakness of signatures is not that they are “not AI.” Failure occurs when the selected invariant changes, is easy to manipulate, or represents a family rather than the malicious capability.

Signatures remain valuable at machine scale and for known threats. Newer models often complement rather than eliminate them.

## Transition

“Learned detectors expand the invariant into a combination of many extracted properties. First, look at where those properties come from.”
