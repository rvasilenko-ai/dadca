# Slide 22 — Approximately 17,600 recovered actions

**Timing:** 55–58 minutes  
**Module:** Why autonomous attacks are noisy

## On-slide content

Hugging Face reconstructed:

- Approximately **17,600 attacker actions**
- Approximately **6,280 action clusters**
- Activity spanning several days
- Thousands of reconnaissance, execution, and staging actions
- Many paths that produced no useful result

> Coverage, not elegance, found the viable chain.

## Speaker notes

State that these are reconstructed counts from available logs, not a guarantee that every action was observed. Hugging Face reports that most actions went nowhere, but the aggregate search found a path across several independent systems.

The key change is the economics of persistence: failed experiments consume compute and time, but much less scarce human attention.

## Transition

“For defenders, the successful path was embedded inside thousands of low-signal events.”
