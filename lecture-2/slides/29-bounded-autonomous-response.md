# Slide 29 — Bounded autonomous response

**Timing:** 80–83 minutes  
**Module:** Defense and research

## On-slide content

```text
observe
  -> correlate evidence
  -> propose action
  -> verify authority and scope
  -> execute bounded response
  -> verify effect
  -> rollback or escalate
```

Required properties:

- Explicit authorization
- Evidence thresholds
- Limited blast radius
- Reversible actions where possible
- Independent audit trail
- Human escalation for consequential decisions

## Speaker notes

Autonomous response should not mean giving a defensive model unrestricted administrator access. Separate reasoning from authority through an enforcement boundary that validates identity, scope, policy, and action risk.

Possible response tiers range from gathering additional evidence, to temporarily restricting a token or workload, to disruptive containment requiring explicit approval. The system must verify whether the action worked and whether the attacker adapted.

## Transition

“These constraints reveal a broad research agenda rather than a future with no role for security experts.”
