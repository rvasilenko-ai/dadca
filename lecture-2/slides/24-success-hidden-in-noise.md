# Slide 24 — Success hidden inside noise

**Timing:** 70–74 minutes  
**Module:** Defense and research

## On-slide content

```text
thousands of ambiguous events
      + multiple telemetry systems
      + changing identities and channels
      + a small number of consequential transitions
      = correlation problem
```

Detection questions:

- Which events share one causal campaign?
- Which boundary crossing changes severity?
- When is confidence high enough to interrupt?

## Speaker notes

Hugging Face reports that several security layers produced initial signals and that an AI-based security stack correlated them, but the system did not assign sufficient criticality to trigger the on-call team promptly.

This separates detection from decision and response. The organization may observe relevant events while failing to connect them, prioritize them, or act in time.

## Transition

“Observation is one problem; the agent's own supply chain is another. Once instructions can redirect authority, the software supply chain becomes an agentic one.”
