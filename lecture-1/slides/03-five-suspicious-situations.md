# Slide 3 — Five suspicious situations

**Timing:** 2–5 minutes  
**Module:** Where is the malware?

## On-slide content

> Where is the malware?

1. A Trojan executable is written to disk.
2. PowerShell downloads and runs code.
3. A PE image exists only in process memory.
4. An attacker controls a legitimate remote-management tool.
5. Repository instructions cause a coding agent to exfiltrate secrets.

Vote for each:

- Definitely malware
- Possibly malware
- Not malware, but part of an attack

## Speaker notes

Collect quick votes and disagreements. Do not resolve them. The purpose is to reveal that students are classifying different things: artifacts, behaviors, intent, or entire systems.

## Interaction

Ask two students who disagreed to explain which property determined their answer.

## Transition

“Hold on to that disagreement; it comes back when we reach detection. First, the route.”
