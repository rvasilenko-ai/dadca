MITRE ATT&CK: what it is

A public, curated knowledge base of adversary behavior, built from observed intrusions, maintained by MITRE since 2015 and updated every April and October.

What is in it
  • Three domains: Enterprise, Mobile, ICS. Enterprise v20.0 (October 2026): 15 tactics, 227 techniques, 479 sub-techniques
  • Procedure examples from documented campaigns; groups, software (malware and tools), campaigns
  • Mitigations, data sources and detections for each technique
What it is for
  • A shared vocabulary for behavior, independent of tool and family names
  • Mapping what a detection pipeline covers, and where the gaps are
  • Structuring threat intelligence, red and purple teaming, product comparison in ATT&CK Evaluations
What it is not
  • A complete list of everything an attacker can do
  • An order of attack: tactics are goals, not steps
  • Evidence: a technique ID names a behavior, it does not observe one

ATT&CK names behaviors. Observing them is the detection pipeline's job, and that is Part 2.
