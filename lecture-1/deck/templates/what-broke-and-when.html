What broke the file-centric model, and when

What broke the modelBecame dominantWhat the defender lost
Diversification of file types: archives, documents, scripts, exploit objects2010–2016, once mail blocked executable attachments; first wave 1995–2001The executable as the main thing to scan
Process injection, reflective loading, memory-resident executionEnd of 2010–2016; standard 2017–2022A file to detonate
Living-off-the-land tools2017–2022; the whole campaign by 2023A malicious artifact at all
Stolen identities, tokens and cloud APIs2017–2022 onwardThe host as the place where the attack happens
Trusted vendor and update channels2017–2022Provenance as a proxy for safety
Tool-using agents and external instructions2025–2026A program as the thing that executes intent

Each break was partly a consequence of defensive success against the previous one.

The money and motivation moved too: from the victim's machine, to the victim's credentials, to the victim's organization, to the victim's identity and data.
