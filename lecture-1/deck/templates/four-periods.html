Four periods, 2000–2026

2000–2009the executable, from worms to botnetsAn executable, mailed to victims or spread by worms over an unencrypted network; botnets turn the infected machine into a product. Antivirus, mail gateways and IDS inspected files and payloads.
2010–2016APT, exploit kits, files of every kindCampaigns and exploit kits; the deliverable is still a file, no longer only an executable. The sandbox is the premium control.
2017–2022the access economyAccess is bought, stolen through identities and injected through trusted vendors. EDR goes mainstream and gets attacked.
2023–2026hands-on hacking, edge devices, agentsIntrusion by hand through help desks and stolen sessions, edge devices that run no EDR, then agents acting on planted instructions.

In each period the attacker moved the capability to where the current sensor was not looking, and the money moved with it.
